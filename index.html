Privacy Policy — Builder: Match 3

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Builder: Match 3 (the "App", package name com.stackattack.reborn) handles information. The App is a single-player puzzle game. You can play it without an internet connection, and it has no accounts, no advertising and no purchases. It does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which is what this policy explains.

Data That Stays On Your Device

The following is stored locally and never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the App:

  • Your best scores and which builders you have unlocked
  • An unfinished game, so you can continue it later
  • Preferences such as the chosen language and whether you have seen the tutorial

Data Sent To Google

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google. Through them the App collects:

  • Gameplay events. When a game starts and ends, the score reached, how long it lasted, how it ended, how many crates were carried, which bonuses were collected, and which step of the tutorial was reached.
  • An app instance identifier. A random identifier generated by Firebase for this installation of the App. It is not your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and it cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics. If the App crashes, a report is sent containing the technical cause, the App version, and device information such as the model, operating system version and available memory at that moment.
  • Approximate location. Derived from your IP address at the country or region level. The App never requests precise location and has no location permission.

Why This Data Is Collected

Two reasons only:

  • To understand how the game is played — for example, at which score players usually stop, or which tutorial step is confusing — so that the game can be improved.
  • To find and fix crashes.

This data is not used for advertising, personalisation or profiling, and it is never sold.

Who Processes This Data

Google processes it on our behalf as a service provider. It is not shared with any other third party. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics data is retained for a limited period (currently up to 14 months) and is then deleted automatically; crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

What The App Does NOT Do

  • No advertising, no advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifier — the App does not request the AD_ID permission at all
  • No in-app purchases and no payment data
  • No accounts, sign-in, email addresses, phone numbers or passwords
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location
  • No selling or renting of data to third parties

Data In Transit

All data described above is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not specifically target children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it collects no personally identifiable information from anyone.

Your Choices

The App has no in-app switch to disable statistics. Uninstalling the App stops all collection. If you would like the data associated with your installation deleted, write to the address below and include the date and approximate time you last played, so it can be located.
