Privacy Policy — Builder: Match 3

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Builder: Match 3 (the "App", package name com.stackattack.reborn) handles information. The App is a single-player puzzle game that runs entirely offline.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The App has no login, account system, email, phone number, or profile of any kind, and it does not connect to the internet.

The only data involved:

  • Local game progress. Your best score, unlocked builders, and in-game preferences (such as language) are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

What We Do NOT Do

  • No internet connection or network requests
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
  • No account creation, email, or password
  • No collection, sharing, or selling of personal data to third parties

Data in Transit

The App works fully offline and does not send any data off your device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It is intended for a general audience and does not specifically target children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
